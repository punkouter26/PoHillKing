Trump learns to fight, and three tries at a signature style

Toddler

He learned to push another Trump off the hill. Teaching him a special way of pushing did not work.

Child

fight1 pushes a standing Trump off 97.5 % of the time. A chest-only reward and a leaning 'bull charge' (switched on at once, then eased in) all made him weaker or made him avoid contact.

Adult

fight1 = r4att (400 it) then trump_r4bull (300 it): belly_push 4, arm_contact -1, push_out 1, vmax 0.7. fight2 = belly_push 8, arm_contact -4, push_out 0. bull1/bull2 = styled action path with obs.BULL_POSE (torso 0.3 rad forward, arms back), bull2 with style_ramp_iters 300.

Cross-body training: Trump and Kim against G1

Toddler

Trump and Kim had only ever practised against copies of themselves. Practising against the robots has not worked yet.

Child

Against the G1 robots their brains win about a third of duels. The first try trained against robots that fell over by themselves; the second learned to stall.

Adult

Mixed scenes (build_mjcf mix), per-robot body data in the env. xg1: stochastic frozen G1 (rebuilt actors std 0.3), no critic warm-up. xg2: deterministic G1, critic warm-up 20; draw = loss = -10. v3: standing-G1 attacker stage, then the league, draw -20 (results below when finished).
