Mountain map upgrade

261019. GFX & Audio option 8. Blender 5.2.2 LTS (Microsoft Store build) run headless on tools/make_mountain_map.py; no Blender MCP server is connected to this session, so the scene was built by the repo's own generator script, which is the map's source of truth. Render only: the collision dome is untouched, the summit inside 1.9 m equals it in every level of detail, and no prop stands within 3.2 m of the centre (banners 3.4 m). Captures are camera renders without the HUD; the overview is ParityBatch.MapOverview.

1. The whole hill

2. From the game's cameras

The broadcast camera frames the summit from Unity z = -6.2, so props were kept off that side (CAMERA_SIDE in the script): they stand behind the fight and never between the camera and a fighter.

3. Banners in the wind

FX/Banner.shader moves the cloth in the vertex stage: a wave travelling out along the flag and a faster flutter, both growing with the distance from the pole, the flag pushed downwind, and the shading normal tilted by the wave's slope so the folds read as moving light and dark bands. MatchFx publishes the wind as the global _KothWind: a breeze of 0.3 (plus up to 0.5 from the live weather's wind speed), 1.3 along the gust during a Hazards gust (measured: 0.30 calm, 1.30 one second into a gust, back to 0.30 after it).

4. Levels of detail and baked shading

MeshVerticesNotes
Mountain_LOD051 265As before: dense rings from the plateau rim to 6 m.
Mountain_LOD115 409Every ring inside 2.5 m kept, every second ring and angle beyond. The Low quality preset forces it (QualityTier calls LODGroup.ForceLOD(1)).
Mountain_LOD25 113Every fourth beyond 2.5 m; far views only.
Crags / Ruins / Banners + poles11 805 / 811 / 351 + 222Box-mapped UVs; caps triangulated so the FBX carries tangents.

Unity's FBX importer makes the three _LODn meshes a LODGroup by itself. Forcing LOD2 for a frame changes the shading over the whole hill while the separate rock meshes stay pixel-identical, so the switch works. Ambient occlusion is baked in Cycles (48 samples, 1.2 m reach) from the hill, the crags, ruins, rocks and poles into the mask's occlusion channel: mean 0.87 over the hill, 5th percentile 0.56. The first bake read 0.25 because LOD1 and LOD2 lie on the same surface and occluded everything; they are now hidden while it bakes.
