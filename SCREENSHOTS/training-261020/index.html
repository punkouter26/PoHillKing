Ten calibration, action, reward and curriculum options: audits of every shipped brain, six G1 fine-tunes of the champion that each change one thing (duel_u1 is the unchanged control), the success-gated opponent ladder for Trump and Kim against the G1 league, and two body fixes. G1: one change per run against the same recipe (options 4-8 and the clip follow-up)

Toddler

We tried six new ways to teach the robot to push. None of them beat the robot we already had.

Child

Each try changed one thing (how far a joint may move, how actions are squashed, how the ring is rewarded, random tactics, no reward clip). The best one, a per-joint movement range, was only as good as the plain retraining; the others lost most of their duels against the champion.

Adult

400-iteration fine-tunes of careful1 at 2048 worlds with duel_u1's pool and rewards. scale1 (per-joint action scale from careful1's raw p95, folded exactly into the warm start) is level with the control (50/45 vs duel_u1) but loses 35/63 to careful1; mask1 (tanh + torque window) 10/85; pbrs1 (potential instead of ring shaping) 19/80; tactic1 8/85 at Press play; noclip1 29/58. Changing a trained brain's action path or reward shaping costs more than 400 iterations recover.

Audits and calibration (options 1, 2, 3, 7)

Toddler

We checked how the fighters move, what they see and what they are rewarded for, and fixed two small body problems.

Child

The robot brains pushed their joints to the limit a lot; part of the reward was quietly thrown away; Kim's feet overlapped a little and Trump stood slightly crooked. The feet and the stance are fixed, and the fighters play exactly as well as before.
