Training report 261019: get-up brain

Rung getup (G1, flat floor): a brain that takes over while a fighter is down and stands it up again, for the game's get-up rule. RTX 2060, 4096 envs, about 3.8 s per PPO iteration. Charts: training curves from TensorBoard (10-iteration means). Evaluation: deterministic actor on 256 drops without the assist (GPU, with domain randomisation), CPU MuJoCo getup_stats.py, the Unity gates. Generated by training/scripts/run_report.py.

Getting up: from sitting forever to standing in under two seconds (getup1 -> getup3)

Toddler

The robot first only learned to sit up. With a helping hand that slowly let go, it learned to stand up all by itself.

Child

Dropped in a random pose, the final brain stands up in every one of 200 test drops, usually in about 1.5 s, well inside the game's 5-second count.

Adult

Without help PPO settled into sitting (getup1); a pull only while low from that sitting policy did not move it (getup2). From scratch with a full body-weight pull on the pelvis fading to zero over 1000 iterations (HoST-style) it stands unaided: graduated at mean reward 28.7 (bar 18), no NaN gradients, 115 min.
